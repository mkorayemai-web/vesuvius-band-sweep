# Review log

Per-sheet record of the visual review behind [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md): 110 sheets, 557 windings.

- A sheet holds 1-22 windings (small inner windings share a sheet). Each tile shows one winding: its reading side
  above its blind side, each the mean of the winding's four maps (2 models x d = +1, +3), stretched to its 1-99 %
  range, at 150-200 µm per pixel.
- The first tile of every sheet is the PHerc0343 known-text control. A sheet counts only if the control's letter rows
  are legible on it ("control legible").
- Rule, fixed before the review: a CANDIDATE needs at least two roughly parallel rows of discrete, stroke-like marks
  over at least 1 cm that are not explained by map borders, cracks, fibres or blocky mesh edges. A feature at the same
  place on consecutive windings is structure.
- Reviewer: the vision model of the AI agent that ran this work. The sheet images are not published (they are ink
  maps of unread scrolls); sheet ids are internal labels.
- Dates: PHerc0813 z7000-10000 and PHerc0257 z12000 on 2026-10-16; PHerc0813 z10000 on 2026-10-22; PHerc0257
  z9000-12000 re-reviewed on 2026-10-22, because the viewer had shrunk the 2026-10-14/15 sheets 2-4x (those are not
  counted).
